FieldScan Pro: Privacy

FieldScan Pro is a personal field-work app for construction site records. This page explains what it does with your information.

What stays on your phone

Your tasks, inspections, photos, voice notes, diary entries and expenses are stored on your own phone, in the app's own storage. The developer does not receive them and has no server that holds them.

Google Drive

If you choose to connect Google Drive, the app signs in with your Google account and reads and writes only the sync files in the Drive folder you name (default: "FieldScanPro Sync"), so your phone and your desktop can stay in step. The sign-in details are kept only on your phone. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Sync screen), or remove access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

What is not collected

No analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no sharing or selling of data.
